## 2. Evidence classification rules

**Source coverage:** COVERED means the cited assertions exercise the stated candidate behavior; PARTIAL means a material BL-03 variant or assertion is absent; NOT VERIFIED means the inspected source does not establish it. **Execution evidence:** VERIFIED requires independently available exact-case output at H63; REPORTED means a run is claimed but method-level logs are unavailable here; NOT AVAILABLE means no applicable record was found. **Acceptance:** ACCEPTED requires approved policy, execution evidence and sign-off; OPEN denotes unresolved case/policy evidence; BLOCKED denotes a required gate. Source presence is never an executed PASS.
